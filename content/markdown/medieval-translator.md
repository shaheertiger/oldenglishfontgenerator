## How the translator works

The tool swaps modern words for their archaic and Early Modern English counterparts — _you_ becomes _thou_, _your_ becomes _thy_, _have_ becomes _hath_, _please_ becomes _prithee_, and _hello_ becomes _well met_. Capitalization is kept, and any word it doesn’t recognize is left untouched so your sentence still reads.

## Authentic, or just for fun?

This is a stylized, for-fun translator rather than a scholarly one. Real Old English (the language of _Beowulf_) and Early Modern English (the language of Shakespeare) are different stages of the language with their own grammar. The substitutions here capture the _flavor_ of medieval and Elizabethan speech — great for D&D campaigns, fantasy writing, renaissance-faire captions, and themed invitations.
