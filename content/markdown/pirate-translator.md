Turn everyday English into salty pirate speak — _ahoy_, _matey_, _ye_, _booty_, _avast_. Type a sentence and copy your buccaneer version. Perfect for Talk Like a Pirate Day, party invites, and themed captions.

Example: “Hello my friend, where is the treasure?” → “Ahoy me matey, where be th' booty?”

## How it works

The translator swaps common words for their pirate equivalents — _hello_ becomes _ahoy_, _you_ becomes _ye_, _your_ becomes _yer_, _friend_ becomes _matey_, and _treasure_ becomes _booty_. Capitalization is preserved, and words it doesn’t recognize are left alone so your sentence still makes sense.

## Where pirate speak comes from

The exaggerated “arr, matey” pirate accent owes more to Hollywood than to history — much of it traces back to actor Robert Newton’s West Country performance in _Treasure Island_. It stuck, and now it’s the go-to voice for pirate-themed fun, especially every September 19th on International Talk Like a Pirate Day.
